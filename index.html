Garden to cup

From the garden to your cup

Shiwani Tea is manufactured and packed by Star Tea Co. in Siliguri, in the Darjeeling district of West Bengal: right in India’s tea country.

  1. Tea gardens

    Every blend starts with tea leaves from India’s tea gardens. Premium draws on the finest tea estates.

  2. CTC

    Crush, tear, curl: the leaf becomes the granular CTC tea that brews a strong, deep chai.

  3. Blended

    Leaves are blended for a cup that tastes the same every time. Gold is 100% blended.

  4. Packed in Siliguri

    Sealed into Shiwani pouches by Star Tea Co. in Matigara, Siliguri.

  5. Your cup

    Boil, brew, pour, sip. Taste the serenity.

How to brew

Make it kadak

  1. Boil 1 cup of water. Add crushed elaichi or ginger now if you like.
  2. Add 1 to 1½ teaspoons of Shiwani Tea and simmer for a minute or two for colour.
  3. Pour in ½ cup of milk and sugar to taste. Let it rise to a rolling boil, twice.
  4. Strain and serve hot. Kulhad optional, smile mandatory.

A simple home method: adjust the tea, milk and boil time to your taste. With Elaichi Chai, you can skip the extra cardamom.
